Top-level taxonomy

  • Code framework and governing standards ? Choose the applicable accessibility authority, code edition, and governing framework before applying technical criteria.
  • Accessible parking and EV charging ? Separate accessible-parking scoping and layout from EV charging requirements; verify the controlling edition.
  • Accessible routes and site circulation ? Evaluate route continuity, site arrival, circulation, transitions, grades, and route-specific conditions.
  • Scoping and alteration triggers ? Determine facility/project applicability, scope of work, alteration obligations, and path-of-travel triggers.
  • Building elements and door/threshold requirements ? Apply clear-opening, maneuvering, threshold, hardware, and approach requirements to the project facts.
  • Plumbing and toilet-room requirements ? Distinguish fixture counts and scoping from the technical requirements for accessible plumbing elements.
  • Housing and federal law comparison ? Compare housing-specific requirements and federal/state overlap without treating standards as interchangeable.
  • CASp responsibility and California law ? Review California CASp program, statutory context, inspection, documentation, and reporting responsibilities.

Key idea

The map stores stable topic IDs, descriptions, and reciprocal test_bank_links to question batches. Each question record carries knowledge_node_ids so the bank and taxonomy remain linked without copying question content into the database.
